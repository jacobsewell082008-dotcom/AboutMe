About Me

A student with a passion for history.

My name is Jacob Sewell, and I am a senior at McCreary Central High School with a 3.83 weighted GPA.

I have always been interested in history, especially Early American History. A dual-credit history course through Eastern Kentucky University helped strengthen that interest and inspired me to pursue history at the college level.

Outside of academics, I enjoy leadership, technology, and community service. Through TSA, school technology projects, church, and volunteer work, I have had the opportunity to work with others and contribute to my community.

Why History?

Early American History

My interest in history began at a young age and has continued to grow throughout my education. I am especially interested in Early American History and the people, ideas, events, and conflicts that shaped the United States.

As an elementary school student, I was selected to participate in a meeting with state legislators, giving me an early opportunity to engage with government and civic affairs.

Later, a dual-credit history course through Eastern Kentucky University with Mr. Joe Waters strengthened my interest in historical research and encouraged me to consider studying history in college.

“Understanding where we came from helps us better understand where we are going.”
